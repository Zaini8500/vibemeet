# Privacy Policy — TEMPLATE (get legal review before publishing)

VibeMeet is an 18+ random 1-to-1 video chat app.

**Data we collect:** account email, display name, date of birth (used only to verify you are 18+, never shown to other users), country, languages, interests, profile photo, and call metadata (match times, duration, reports). We do **not** record video calls by default and do not store call video.

**Data we share:** with your TURN provider (to relay video when P2P fails), Google Play Billing (for purchases), and a moderation provider (only sampled metadata/verdicts when a report is filed, if enabled).

**Your rights:** you can edit your profile, block/report users, and delete your account in-app (Settings → Delete my account) or via the web deletion link. Deletion removes your profile and personal data, subject to legal retention for safety reports.
